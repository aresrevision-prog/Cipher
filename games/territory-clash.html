How to play

  • ⭐Pick your capital. Tap any land to found your empire. It earns gold and troops — defend it.
  • ⚔Choose a weapon, then tap where to strike. Land pushes your border into a neighbour; Boat sails across water; Plane flies anywhere. Your land front then keeps itself supplied — troops and new growth flow to it automatically until it's spent or you redirect with a new tap.
  • %Set how much to send. The % buttons decide how big each push is — and for Boats/Planes that share is the troops you load aboard.
  • ⛃Spend gold in the Tech panel. Research Naval & Aviation, then build Shipyards, Airfields, Forts, Barracks and Banks.
  • 🧱Walls, bridges & tunnels. Walls fortify a tile so enemies stall on it. Bridges lay over the sea tile-by-tile so your army walks across; tunnels bore straight to the far shore. Breaking a crossing sinks it.
  • ☢Missile strikes. Build a Silo, then Launch and tap a target: the blast flattens enemy walls and forts and wipes out the troops caught in it — your way to crack a turtle. Silos need to reload between shots.
  • ⚓Buy boats & planes. You stockpile them at your buildings; each strike spends one. Deploy a Carrier at sea to launch from mid-ocean.
  • 🗺Use the minimap (bottom-right) to jump around big maps. Scroll/pinch to zoom, drag to pan.
  • 🏆Win by eliminating every rival empire.
